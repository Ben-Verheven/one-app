Mark's decisions for the Call Board since it began to take in every call,
not only Horizon's (2026-10-24). PROJECT.md says how the room works; this
says what Mark chose and turned down, so a later session does not quietly
undo it, and what was agreed and is not built yet.

## What it is for

- **A call database, read on the Call Board** (2026-10-24): every call
  Mark follows, the Horizon Work Programme's and the arenas', each with
  its dates, its official text and a way in from the Ledger.
- **Calls only, brokerage in the Semaphore** (2026-10-25): call dates and
  everything about the calls land here; brokerage events and venues land
  in the Semaphore, their proper house, and a venue's card there keeps its
  slice of the Call Board, the calls it is for drawn as here. The tab row stays the way in, one view to a tab: Mark likes
  choosing a Horizon cluster in one press and wants the same for the rest.
- Nothing may clutter it: every addition was drawn first and chosen
  against at least one quieter way.
- **A triangle chooses the row** (issue 1, 2026-10-28): one row of tabs
  in the beam at a time, chosen by a three-pointed triangle beside it
  that turns until the point pressed faces right, like the line of room
  marks: Horizon (a sun on the horizon), Partnerships (an EU star) and
  All & Freezer (a frost crystal), the group's name beside it. Chosen
  over letters at the points. It replaced two rows standing at once
  (2026-10-25), each named at its left, which had replaced a roll of
  2026-10-24 whose peeking rows were cut off at the bar's edges and "did
  not at all work nicely"; the thirteen in one row ran into the room's
  name on the iPad held sideways; three words choosing the row, with no
  roll, was turned down that day.
- **No counts on the tabs** (2026-10-28): the number of topics or calls
  on each tab went from the beam, on Mark's word. The count under the
  search still stands while a search or a choice narrows the list.

## Horizon tabs

## Tabs beyond Horizon

- **JU, CoFund, CoProg and OTHER** (2026-10-24, written so since
  2026-10-28): a tab each for Mark's
  three big types, Joint Undertakings, co-funded and co-programmed
  partnerships, and one for every other arena. Pillar 2 was the fourth
  main type and goes into the cluster tabs. The types are told apart by
  their names (`TYPE_TABS` in callboard.js), so a type renamed away from
  those words moves to OTHER.
- **The arena is the chip** (2026-10-24): one row to a call, the arena's
  name on the coloured chip and the call in the text beside it, the shape a
  Horizon topic has. Chosen over
  the arena's name once beside all its calls, like a merged table cell,
  and over a light line heading each arena. A dark heading per arena, as
  a destination has, was turned down: most arenas hold a call or two, and
  a heading each cluttered the sheet and cost the chart a row.
- **The chip names the arena, not its kind** (2026-10-28): "European
  Partnership for", "Co-funded Partnership", "Joint Undertaking" and the
  like are left off the chips (`tagName` in callboard.js), since they told
  no partnership from another and were in the way. The full name stays on
  hover, in a search and in its panel.
- **The Semaphore's colours and tags** (issue 2, 2026-10-28): an arena
  wears its type's colour from Types, its name in the Semaphore's tag (a
  wash with a solid edge at its left, in italic), so the two rooms read as
  one. A call is lighter the further off it is, on its bar and on its
  tag alike: full within three months, lighter beyond six and twelve.
  Mark chose that over full for the round a call is in and pale for the
  next. Before, each arena had a colour of its own here. Since a tab's
  arenas may now share one colour, the rule between arenas is firmer.
- **A date not yet published to the day is drawn where it can fall**
  (2026-10-28): a soft band over the month, quarter or half it is known
  to, the bar solid only between what is known, the hard tick and the dot
  kept for a date to the day, and ≈ before its words. It was a dashed,
  see-through bar, which put a deadline known only to its month on that
  month's last day with a real deadline's tick. Chosen over a hollow bar
  for anything uncertain. The Semaphore's slices of the Call Board, on a
  venue's and an arena's panel, draw it the same way.
- **An arena's tab is the chart alone** (2026-10-24): the list beside it
  went, as it only said again what the chart says, cut short. The chart
  takes the width, each chip as long as its arena's name, which tells one
  arena from another at a glance, and the call named more fully beside it;
  the dates stand on the chart and on hover. What a search finds in the
  other tabs stands under the chart.
- **Pressing a call or its arena opens the whole arena where it stands**
  (2026-10-24): a frame round its own rows, which keep their chips, names
  and bars where they were, the board's month lines showing through it
  (2026-10-28), and over them one line: its name and type, **Site** and
  **Reference** (its first link to a page and its first to a document),
  Seen, its panel and ✕. The other arenas dim. Its dates show on hover, as
  on any row. Chosen on 2026-10-28 over a month line of the frame's own
  and over a frame opening upwards over the arena above. Its About, its
  count of open leads and its dates written on every bar stood in it until
  then; Mark found them in the way. A click outside it shuts it and does
  nothing else, as do ✕ and Esc. Chosen over opening it alone with the
  others put away, which lost its place among them.
- **Dates said once** (2026-10-25): Mark found them "often superfluous".
  A call's dates stand on its bar, on hover, lit or in an arena opened,
  and nowhere else beside it; the line under the chart that said them
  again for an arena's call went. Where an opening's tag and a deadline's
  would overlap on a short bar, the opening's gives way.
- **The Freezer is a tab** (issue 1, 2026-10-28), beside All: every

- **Types in OTHER, named once** (2026-10-24): where a tab holds arenas of

## Judging finds (built 2026-10-24)

- **One at a time, before they land.** The board goes to the tab where a
  find belongs, draws it there with a dashed edge, and opens it as the
  panel it will become (2026-10-28; a dark bar along the foot of the room
  until then): its site under its name, and in the green box a suggested
  arena Add, in a type chosen there and guessed from its words, or No. Back and Skip go round the
  finds still waiting, in the order of the tabs; Stop leaves them, counted
  on the pill in the search row, which starts again, and dotted on their
  tabs.
- **Counted in the ring** (2026-10-28): a small brass number beside the
  Call Board's mark, wherever Mark is, the new arenas and call texts
  waiting. Drawn first inside the mark, it came out as faint as the mark
  of a room not shown, so it stands beside it.
- The finds are the new arenas ChatGPT's search for calls suggests, which
  wait on the board itself (`suggested`) until judged, and the agent's,
  from its inbox in the cloud; and the official call texts (2026-10-28),
  below. New venues were judged here too until they went back to the
  Semaphore (2026-10-25).
- **Dates are not judged**: new and changed ones go straight on and glow on
  their call's row until Seen there, which
  marks the whole arena seen as its panel does; a changed date says on hover
  what it was. A Pillar 2 arena's dates are the Work Programme's, which the
  Horizon check keeps, below; no search writes them since 2026-10-28.
- **ChatGPT's button for calls is here** (2026-10-25), in the controls,
  and the Semaphore's is the search for brokerage: each search has its

## The three searches and the Horizon check (built 2026-10-28)

The first phase agreed on 2026-10-24, drawn on the canvas named above
and chosen there. How they work is at "ChatGPT's search" in semaphore.js
and in the Horizon check's own file; what Mark chose:

- **Each finds one thing.** Calls: the call dates of every arena but the
  clusters' and missions', and the few arenas missing, each with its kind,
  its call dates and two quotes, one showing a call for international
  consortia, one that Dutch organisations can join. Brokerage: only venues
  where projects in the making are described, a quote saying so on each.
  Call texts: the texts of calls on the board, word for word. A frozen
  arena is asked only whether a new call is announced, and left out of
  brokerage. No search writes a cluster's or mission's dates.
- **The searches overdid it**, and Mark asked that they stop (2026-10-28):
  calls and brokerage for international consortia TU/e can join, project
  descriptions, never "getting to know one another". On his real board 43
  of 93 venues brought had been removed, most of them webinars, info days
  and conferences, and 53 call dates were guesses written as days with
  "(expected)" in the name. He chose firmer words and **a gate in the app**
  that leaves out, saying why, what breaks the rules, over words alone and
  over a ceiling of finds per round. A doubt written into a call's name is
  taken out again and the date made uncertain.
- **Call texts come from the portal first**: the Horizon check copies the
  text of every call whose name carries an EU portal id (the Joint
  Undertakings', EIC, MSCA, WIDERA and the like), exact and free, and
  ChatGPT brings only the rest. Chosen over ChatGPT for all. The parts
  that matter, word for word, with the whole a link away, over the whole
  text. Each is judged like any find before it is kept; reading it from
  the panel puts the panel aside until the reader is shut.
- **A kept text opens from Text beside its call in an arena opened**, the
  quieter way, over a mark on every row of the chart.
- **The Horizon check is a plain program**, as Mark's Place's copier is,
  on Supabase's schedule, using no Claude. What the portal says is kept
  apart and laid over the Work Programme, which is never changed. A topic
  gone from the portal, or moved to another call, is **struck through
  where it stands**, glowing until Seen and struck for good, chosen over
  folding such topics into one line. A call the portal dates otherwise is
  drawn at the portal's dates, glowing until Seen, the Work Programme's on
  hover. Only the portal's notices about dates, eligibility or a
  cancellation stand in the reader; topics new to the portal are left out.
- Two lessons from its first reading (2026-10-28): the portal's search,
  asked for many calls at once, leaves out a sixth of the topics, so it is
  asked call by call and a topic is gone only when asking for it by its
  own id finds nothing too; and a title reused in next year's call is no
  move, so a topic has moved only when its namesake is new to the portal.
