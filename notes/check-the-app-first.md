When a brief says a new thing should use the shape of an existing part of
the app, look at that part running before designing. Mark caught the Notes
room having invented its own reading of "four-deep like the Books page" —
headings and a bare list — when the real page is file tabs over one sheet,
coloured rows, and note-name buttons that balloon the note open in place.

Why: the words "book, page, row, note" name a layout he reads every day,
not a hierarchy to reinterpret. A described shape and a seen shape are
different things, and only one of them is what he will compare the result
against.

How to apply: photograph it first, then read the module in MAP.md — that
order, never the other one. [testing-this-app](testing.md) has the mechanics for
driving the app to a state the canned screenshots do not reach. (The
Ledger's Books page this story names is gone since; the lesson is not.)
