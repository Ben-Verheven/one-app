Mark's decisions about how the Atlas room reads and moves — Browse, the
map, the side pane and the dossier — as distinct from the graph underneath
([atlas-graph](atlas-graph.md)) and the agents ([atlas-agent](atlas-agent.md)).

**Browse shows every declared keyword, whole, as chips** (#20, chosen
2026-10-28 from a sheet of three). Before, each researcher was one line
with their spreadsheet cell cut off by an ellipsis — "all keywords from a
researcher should be visible instead of the dots". Rejected: a group
opening into cards two side by side (it stops reading as a list and the
eye zigzags). The chips are the cell cut at its commas, not the declared
layer's attachments, which add every shorter term the build found inside a
keyword and tripled the line with nothing new; the keywords Mark assigned
himself follow in his colour. Each department's colour runs down the side
of its block and washes the groups opened in it; a line held at the top of
the scroll says where you are and takes you back up to any step of it.

**Moving around slides rather than jumps** (#20). A row's children are a
block of their own, opened and shut by height on the 320ms easeInOutCubic
the lead cards use ([animation](animation.md)); a researcher pressed is
marked without redrawing the list. Do not go back to one flat list
redrawn whole.

**The map answers a pointed-at dot with a card** (#20, Mark took this
together with the Browse chips): the name, group and every keyword beside
the dot, following the pointer at the desk and staying on the dot chosen
with a press, which is the iPad's way to it. The three departments are
washed under their dots in the plane's own units. The map's layout — most
people in one dense cluster on the left — was named and left alone.

**The side pane is a strip that slides out over the results** (#14,
2026-10-28). Mark asked for it small by default, coming out on a press or
on hover; of the two he chose both. It waits 180ms under a resting pointer
before coming out and 300ms after the pointer leaves before going, so a
pointer crossing the left edge does not open it and one slipping off its
edge does not lose it; a press anywhere else in the room puts it away, and
on the iPad, with no hover, the press is the way in. Over the results,
never beside them, so nothing in the results moves while it comes and
goes. The pin docks it beside them as it used to stand, per device. On the
map, pressing a dot brings it out, since that is where the chosen
researcher's buttons are. The edits list left the pane for **Edits** in the
bar, a box with the list, undo, Export, Import and Clear all, because it
grew with every edit under settings set once and left.
