write it — `file:line` of the first one, `x N` for how many there are in all. A dash
means nothing was found, and both kinds of dash are worth a second look: paint with
nothing writing it is usually dead, and a name written with no paint is usually a typo.

source and will not be here. That is the case grep is still for, and the second of
the two lists at the foot of this file is where those names gather.
